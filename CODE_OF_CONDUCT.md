# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want a respectful, accessible, disability-led space where people can contribute without needing technical expertise.

## Expected behaviour

- Respect names, pronouns, communication preferences, access needs, and lived experience.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Use plain language and explain technical terms.
- Make room for questions and different ways of contributing.
- Accept correction without demanding that someone disclose private health or disability information.
- Give credit for ideas, testing, writing, design, and code.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Disability stereotypes, cure narratives, or claims that one person speaks for an entire community.
- Pressuring someone to disclose a diagnosis, access need, or private information.
- Publishing private messages or identifying information without permission.
- Deliberately making participation inaccessible after a barrier has been identified.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to an organisation owner. Do not require the person reporting harm to confront the person involved. Owners will limit information to the people needed to review the concern and will document any project action taken.

Organisation owners may remove content, limit participation, or remove access when needed to protect contributors and the project.
