# Governance

## Purpose

The Unnamed AI Group maintains an open collection of AI skills developed with and for people with disabilities.

## Roles

- Contributors propose ideas, report problems, test skills, and submit changes.
- Maintainers review contributions, protect project safety, and merge approved changes.
- Organisation owners manage membership, repository settings, and maintainer appointments.

## Decisions

- Use an issue for a new problem, goal, policy question, or large change.
- Use a pull request for a specific proposed change.
- Record the final decision and its reasons in the issue or pull request.
- Seek relevant lived-experience review for material accessibility decisions.
- If agreement is not possible, an organisation owner makes the temporary decision and records why. The group may revisit it when new evidence appears.

## Review and merging

At least one maintainer reviews each pull request. A contributor must not be the only reviewer of their own material change.

After a second active maintainer is appointed, enable branch protection for `main`, require a pull request, and require at least one approval. Do not enable a rule that could leave the repository with nobody able to merge urgent corrections.

## Membership

Public contributors do not need organisation membership. Owners may invite regular contributors. Give the least access needed and review access when roles change.

## Changes to governance

Propose governance changes in an issue and a pull request. Allow time for contributors to review an accessibility or participation impact before merging.
