# Example skill

## Goal

Complete the requested task while keeping the person in control and recording what was tested.

## Instructions

1. Confirm the requested outcome and any accessibility or format preferences that affect the work.
2. Use short steps, descriptive headings, meaningful link text, and controls identified by name.
3. Do not rely only on colour, position, shape, sound, or an image to communicate meaning.
4. Do not collect or expose passwords, access tokens, private transcripts, medical information, or confidential client information.
5. Ask for clear confirmation before a high-impact or hard-to-reverse action.
6. State what was tested, which AI product or model was used, and what remains uncertain.

## Success checks

- The requested outcome is complete.
- The person can review and change the result.
- Safety and privacy boundaries were followed.
- Accessibility claims are limited to what was actually tested.
- Failures and corrections are recorded rather than hidden.

## Limits

Do not claim that one result works for every person with a disability or behaves identically in every AI product.
